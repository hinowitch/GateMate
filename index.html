What is GateMate?

Building custom FPV gates for Tiny Whoops or 5-inch quads usually means dealing with heavy metal bases, expensive retail gear, or unstable frames. GateMate changes that. By combining precision 3D-printed connectors with budget-friendly, lightweight PVC pipes from your local hardware store, you can snap together a professional race track in minutes.
